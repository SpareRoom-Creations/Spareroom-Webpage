About

Hi, I'm Judy.

Turning complex business challenges into clear strategies, practical programs and communications that help people take action has been at the heart of my career.

My path has run from internal communications and marketing at Telstra, through my own consultancy, to leading AI enablement and strategic communications at Salesforce across ANZ and APAC. Along the way I've brought together Sales, Customer Success, Marketing, Pre-Sales and Partners, helping teams find and quantify AI opportunities and turn large volumes of customer intelligence into insight leaders can act on.

I work where strategy, people and technology meet. Whether it's introducing new AI capabilities, helping a team through change or rethinking how an internal comms function works, I start by understanding the real business problem, then design a practical way forward.

At my core, I'm a problem solver. I like taking something complex or undefined, working out what's really needed, and creating the structure, tools and momentum that help people make better decisions.

Spare Room Creations is where that instinct goes after hours: a spare room where I build the tools I wish existed. It's my testing ground for new ideas, and it's why my advice is grounded in things I've actually built.
